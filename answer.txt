Q1) What are some pros and cons of Agile Development (vs Non Agile Development)? List 2 each.
A1) Pros of Agile development include smaller cross functional teams, constant collaboration with customers, and being more resilient to scope changes in the 
Project. Cons of non agile development include delayed testing, no value to customer till the end, and dynamic changes are not possible.

Q2) Explain the roles and responsibilities of the Scrum Master Vs the Product Owner
A2) Scrum Master does all scrum related tasks including JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings, and standups. Product Owners sets direction for the 
team, handles release management and stakeholder management.
